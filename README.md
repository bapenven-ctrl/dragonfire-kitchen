Dragonfire Kitchen: you are Mina, hearth-keeper of a dragon inn. Grill each dragon's order over its own fire-breath before its patience burns out — 8 dragon recipes, 12 ingredients, 1 fire-breath grill, a 180-second shift. Three named dragons order from you: Pyra the patient (she saves up for a Hoard Feast), Ash the impatient, and Bramble in between. Pick ingredients from the bins, grill them on four slots, assemble the dish on the plate, and serve it to the right dragon before its patience runs out. Serve in a row to build a combo; fill the fire-breath meter to finish everything on the grill at once.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click bins to pick up ingredients, click a grill slot to cook, click the plate to add, click a ticket to serve. Keys: 1–0 pick bins, Q/W/E/R grill slots, Space serve, B fire-breath, T clear tray, R restart.
